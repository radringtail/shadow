Running from the Start

I have actively watched Games Done Quick events since the mid-2010s and even attended AGDQ 2020 in Orlando. During COVID, I ran out of projects and was looking for something new. When I decided to try streaming, speedrunning was an obvious choice to try for the first time. My first game? Phantasy Star Online.

Since then, I've become part of many speedrun communities, volunteered for Fastest Furs, and performed multiple times in front of the in-person GDQ audience. A dream come true in just a few years!
