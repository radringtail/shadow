Ghost Prints

Each year, festival ghosts are drawn to spooky celebrations around the globe. Their activity leaves behind invisible, spectral imprints we call "ghost prints."

For the Peek-a-BOO! Festival, G.H.O.S.T. worked with the Trading Post staff and volunteers to investigate the physical properties of locally-sourced ghost prints and turn them into a fun, engaging activity for festival-goers.

Anatomy of a Ghost Print

Results from previous grant-funded research show that various components of discovered ghost prints point to the motivations, magic and ambitions of these special spectres.

Attraction

The shape of the print helps us understand what draws the ghosts to a specific area or activity at the festival.

In this case, a circle indicates the ghost was attracted to activities causing learning, just as you learned about the areas of the festival while collecting stamps.

Aura

The border pattern adorning the print gives us a sense of what feelings the ghosts enhance when they are present.

Here, a triangle pattern indicates the ghost was enhancing the focus of those in the area with its magic. Could you feel it as you completed the Stamp Rally?

Affinity

The color of the mark left behind helps us understand what the ghosts desire most in their time at the festival.

Interestingly, black ink shows us a desire for a patient soul. Did you have to exercise much patience to complete this task? Or will patience be required soon?
