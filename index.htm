G.H.O.S.T.

Ghost Harmony Outreach & Support Team

G.H.O.S.T. is an international nonprofit that exists to end loneliness in the festival ghost population and all those they naturally seek.

Ghost Prints

Each year, festival ghosts are drawn to spooky celebrations around the globe. Their activity leaves behind invisible, spectral imprints we call "ghost prints."

For the Peek-a-BOO! Festival, G.H.O.S.T. worked with BFC Theming to investigate the physical properties of locally-sourced ghost prints and turn them into a fun, engaging activity for festival-goers.

Anatomy of a Ghost Print

Results from previous grant-funded research show that various compoments of discovered ghost prints point to the motivations, magic and ambitions of these special spectres.

Attraction

Shape, what the ghost is attracted to

Aura

Border, what kind of feelings the ghost embues others with

Affinity

Color, what kind of friend the ghost is looking for
